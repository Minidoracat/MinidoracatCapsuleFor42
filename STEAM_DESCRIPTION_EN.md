[h1]🚗 Minidoracat Vehicle Capsule for B42[/h1]
[h3]By Minidoracat[/h3]

[hr][/hr]

[b]⚠ In development, not released yet.[/b]

Pack a parked vehicle into a capsule, throw the capsule to bring it back. Mods, condition and cargo are all kept.

[h2]📦 Requirements[/h2]
[list]
[*] Required: [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3789836701]Minidoracat UI Library for B42[/url] (listed under Required Items; the mod won't load without it)
[*] Works in singleplayer and multiplayer; in multiplayer the server must enable this mod
[*] [b]Add/remove mid-save:[/b] can be added; before removing, unpack every capsule first, otherwise the packed vehicles and their cargo disappear with the capsules
[*] [b]Languages:[/b] 繁體中文, 简体中文, English, 日本語, 한국어, Русский, Español, Português, Türkçe, Français, Polski, Deutsch (please report any translation issues)
[/list]

[h2]🚀 Quick start[/h2]
[olist]
[*] Get an empty capsule: loot mechanic shops and car supply stores, or get one from an admin or the economy center
[*] Park the vehicle, turn the engine off and leave nobody inside, then right-click it and choose "Pack into capsule"
[*] When you need it, right-click the capsule, choose "Throw capsule", pick a landing spot with the cursor and left-click to throw
[/olist]

[h2]✨ Features[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Pack[/b]: the owner packs a parked vehicle into a capsule; mods, paint, condition, fuel and cargo are all kept, and the capsule is named after the vehicle
[*] [b]Throw to unpack[/b]: the cursor outlines the vehicle; green means it fits, red tells you why it doesn't. The capsule lands in a puff of smoke and the vehicle appears as it was
[*] [b]Too heavy to carry[/b]: if the cargo makes the capsule too heavy to pick up, press its button on the ground and the vehicle appears next to it
[*] [b]Effects[/b]: nearby players see smoke and a flash and hear a sound when vehicles are packed or unpacked; zombies are not attracted or distracted
[*] [b]Tooltip and 3D preview[/b]: hover a capsule to see the vehicle name, condition, fuel, missing parts and cargo; right-click "Preview vehicle" to rotate a 3D view of the vehicle model
[*] [b]How to get capsules[/b]: loot (mechanic shops, car supply stores, gas station repair bays and home garages; admins can toggle each and tune the odds) or admin handouts; with the economy mod installed, capsules can be sold in shops
[*] [b]Records and recovery[/b]: each capsule unpacks only once, and every pack and unpack is logged; if a capsule goes missing, admins can trace it and restore the vehicle
[*] [b]Performance[/b]: packed vehicles are not in the world, so crowded spots run smoother
[/list]
