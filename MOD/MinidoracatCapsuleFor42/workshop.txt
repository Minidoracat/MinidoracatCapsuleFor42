description=[h2]🚀 Quick Start[/h2]
description=[olist]
description=[*] Get an empty capsule: loot mechanic shops and car supply stores, or get one from an admin or the Economy Center
description=[*] Park, turn the engine off and make sure nobody is inside, then right-click the vehicle; or right-click a live animal and open its submenu. Choose [b]Pack into Capsule[/b], or right-click an empty capsule in your inventory and choose [b]Throw to Capture[/b]
description=[*] When you need it, right-click the capsule, choose [b]Throw Capsule[/b], pick a landing spot with the cursor and left-click
description=[*] Capsule too heavy to pick up, or want it out right there? Right-click it on the ground and choose [b]Press the Button[/b]
description=[/olist]
description=
description=[h2]✨ Features[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*] [b]Pack into a capsule[/b]: right-click a vehicle or an animal to pack it into a capsule labeled with its name
description=[*] [b]Throw to capture[/b]: throw an empty capsule at an animal or a vehicle to pack it, even a wild animal that runs from you
description=[*] [b]Throw to bring it back[/b]: the cursor marks the landing spot, the capsule lands in a puff of smoke and the vehicle or animal appears as it was
description=[*] [b]Seven capsule types[/b]: from cartoon and realistic to sci-fi and magic, each with its own smoke and sounds
description=[*] [b]Kept as they were[/b]: a vehicle's mods, condition and cargo, an animal's breed, age, genes and pregnancy
description=[*] [b]Tooltip and 3D preview[/b]: hover a capsule to see a vehicle's condition or an animal's details, and spin a 3D view of a vehicle
description=[*] [b]Safehouse protection[/b]: vehicles and animals in someone else's safehouse can't be packed, and nothing can be released there
description=[*] [b]No copies, nothing lost[/b]: a crash or a rolled-back save never creates an extra vehicle, animal or cargo, and admins can trace a lost capsule and restore what it held
description=[/list]
