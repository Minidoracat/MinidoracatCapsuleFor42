description=[h2]🚀 Quick Start[/h2]
description=[olist]
description=[*] Get an empty capsule: loot mechanic shops and car supply stores, or get one from an admin or the Economy Center
description=[*] Park, turn the engine off, make sure nobody is inside, then right-click the vehicle and choose [b]Pack into Capsule[/b]
description=[*] When you need it, right-click the capsule, choose [b]Throw Capsule[/b], pick a landing spot with the cursor and left-click
description=[*] Capsule too heavy to pick up? Right-click it on the ground and choose [b]Press the Button[/b]
description=[/olist]
description=
description=[h2]✨ Features[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*] [b]Pack into a capsule[/b]: right-click a vehicle to pack it; the capsule carries the vehicle's name
description=[*] [b]Throw to unpack[/b]: the cursor outlines the vehicle, the capsule lands in a puff of smoke and the vehicle appears as it was
description=[*] [b]Kept as it was[/b]: mods, paint, condition, fuel and the cargo all travel with it
description=[*] [b]Tooltip and 3D preview[/b]: hover a capsule to see condition and cargo, and spin a 3D view of the model
description=[*] [b]Crash-safe[/b]: a server crash never leaves you with an extra vehicle or a second copy of the cargo
description=[*] [b]Records and recovery[/b]: every capsule is logged; if one goes missing, admins can trace it and restore the vehicle
description=[*] [b]Several ways to get capsules[/b]: buy them in the Economy Center, get them from admins, or loot them at mechanic spots
description=[/list]
